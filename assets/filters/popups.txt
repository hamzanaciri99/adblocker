!   match a document load, so nothing here can close an ordinary tab.

! Deliberately no TLD-wide rules (`*.top`, `*.click`, ...) here. They catch a
! lot of pop domains, but they also close tabs the user opened on purpose, and a
! blocker that eats real navigations is worse than one that misses a pop. The
! rotating-domain case is covered by the `prevent-popunder` scriptlet instead,
! which only defuses cross-site `window.open` from the page's own script.
