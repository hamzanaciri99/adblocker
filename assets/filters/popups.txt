! Title: Umbra Pop-unders
! Description: Pop-under networks. `$popup` rules are consulted by the popup
!   guard when the browser reports a new navigation target; untyped rules never
!   match a document load, so nothing here can close an ordinary tab.

! --- The general defence ---------------------------------------------------
! No domain scope: this runs everywhere. Pop networks rotate domains faster than
! any list can follow, so the only durable defence is behavioural. The scriptlet
! allows same-site opens and cross-site opens that match a link the user just
! clicked, and swallows the rest. The "Close pop-unders" switch in the toolbar
! popup turns it off, per-site or globally.
##+js(prevent-popunder)
