! Title: Umbra site pack - aniwave
! Domains rotate constantly (aniwave.to / .at / .li / .se / ...), so every rule
! uses the entity form `aniwave.*`. The DNR compiler expands that across a TLD
! list at build time because Chrome has no entity syntax.

! Stand down from broad generic hiding here: this site runs a detector and
! generic `.ad`-style rules would hide its bait (D1).
@@||aniwave.*^$generichide

! --- Network -------------------------------------------------------------
||bidgear.com^$important
||whos.amung.us^$important
||aniwave.*/ads/*$xhr
||aniwave.*/ajax/ads*$xhr

! --- Cosmetic (specific selectors only; never `:remove()`, see D5) --------
aniwave.*##.adx
aniwave.*##.player-ads
aniwave.*##.ads-wrapper
aniwave.*##div[class^="ads-"]
aniwave.*##.watch-ads
aniwave.*##iframe[src*="//ads."]

! --- Scriptlets ----------------------------------------------------------
! `AaDetector` is this site's adblock detector; reading it throws inside its own
! script and nowhere else.
aniwave.*##+js(abort-on-property-read, AaDetector)
! `__pf` is the "interstitial already shown" flag. Pre-setting it is quieter
! than blocking the interstitial.
aniwave.*##+js(set-cookie, __pf, 1)
! Player interaction (play / pause / seek) is what triggers the pop.
aniwave.*##+js(no-window-open-if, _blank)
aniwave.*##+js(prevent-popunder)
aniwave.*##+js(set-constant, adBlockDetected, false)
aniwave.*##+js(set-constant, canRunAds, true)
