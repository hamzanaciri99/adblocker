! --- Generic cosmetic -----------------------------------------------------
! Kept deliberately narrow. Broad `.ad`/`.banner` rules are what hide a site's
! bait element and convict the blocker (D1); site packs add precise selectors.
##.adsbygoogle-noablate
##ins.adsbygoogle:empty
##div[id^="div-gpt-ad"]
##div[id^="google_ads_iframe"]
##iframe[src*="googlesyndication.com"]
##iframe[src*="doubleclick.net"]
##iframe[id^="aswift_"]
##.taboola-container
###taboola-below-article
##div[data-widget-id^="outbrain"]
##.OUTBRAIN
